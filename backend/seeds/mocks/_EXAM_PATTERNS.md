## IBPS PO Mains

Bank Mains is qualitatively different — heavier paper, descriptive section, computer aptitude.

- **Total objective:** ~155 Qs / **180 min** · with sectional timing per below
- **Descriptive:** 2 Qs / **30 min** · 25 marks (Letter Writing + Essay)
- **Total:** 200 marks (objective) + 25 marks (descriptive) = 225 marks

- **Sections:**
  - **Reasoning & Computer Aptitude:** 45 Qs / 60 min · 60 marks
  - **General/Economy/Banking Awareness:** 40 Qs / 35 min · 40 marks
  - **English Language:** 35 Qs / 40 min · 40 marks
  - **Data Analysis & Interpretation:** 35 Qs / 45 min · 60 marks
  - **Descriptive (Letter + Essay):** 2 Qs / 30 min · 25 marks
- **Negative marking:** −0.25 per wrong objective.

**Special notes:**
- **Reasoning + Computer Aptitude** is a combined section: ~30 reasoning Qs (puzzles, seating, input-output, syntactical reasoning) + ~15 Computer Aptitude Qs (basics of OS, MS Office, internet, data structures, networks, security, hardware).
- **Banking Awareness:** RBI policies, monetary policy, banking terminology, financial market institutions, current banking news (last 6 months), agricultural & priority-sector lending, KYC norms, NPA classification, etc.
- **Data Analysis:** harder than DI in Prelims. Multi-set caselets, pie+bar combos, missing-data DI.

## Quality benchmarks (matching Testbook/Oliveboard/Adda247)

For every Q across every stage, we must hit:

1. **Topic accuracy:** Every Q's topic must match what the actual exam tests. No CGL Q in a Bank paper. No reasoning trivia outside the syllabus.
2. **Difficulty calibration:** Per the gradient — Mock-01 starts foundational; Mock-25 hits exam-day hard. NEVER beyond syllabus.
3. **Self-verified math/logic:** Every Quant/Reasoning Q solved end-to-end before commit. Distractors must be specific miscomputations (not random wrong numbers).
4. **Realistic stems:** RC passages, DI caselets, puzzles must match the length and density of real Mains-day paper.
5. **PYQ-pattern fidelity:** Topic distribution per section must match the last 3 years of real papers (e.g., Bank Pre Quant has 10-15 DI Qs out of 35 — every mock must reflect that).
6. **Banking/Railway-specific GA:** Bank mocks need RBI policies, monetary aggregates, banking terminology. RRB mocks need Indian Railways heritage facts and NTPC-specific GA.
7. **Sectional-timing enforcement:** For Bank Pre/Mains (and any future sectionally-timed exam), the JSON must carry per-section `time_limit_minutes` and `has_sectional_timing: true`. The frontend already enforces this via MockTestInterface section locks.
