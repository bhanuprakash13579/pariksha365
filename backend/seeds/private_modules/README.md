# AP TET & AP DSC (SGT) — Private Modules

Two email-gated `private_modules` (same universe as EPFO APFC and SSC CGL AAO). Built
from the official October-2025 AP TET notification and the April-2025 AP Mega-DSC (TRT)
notification, plus the Avanigadda "Pragathi" study corpus and the Dec-2025 AP TET
Telugu question papers supplied by the user.

Everything is **chaptered**: a candidate opens one exam section → one chapter → studies
and practises *only* that chapter, exactly like AAO / APFC. No candidate is ever forced
to face the whole syllabus at once. A separate **Full Mock** section holds full-length,
exactly-timed papers.

Official notifications archived at `~/Documents/AP_TET_SGT_Official/`.

Both TET and SGT test generic Mathematics (number system, percentage, SI/CI, algebra,
geometry, mensuration, time-speed-distance) and English (grammar, vocabulary,
comprehension) — the *same* skills the main Pariksha365 quiz app already drills at far
greater depth and volume:

| Reasoning | 4,419 Qs |

| Vocabulary | 2,892 Qs |

Every quant/English topic type-checked against these modules (Simple Interest,
Percentage, HCF/LCM, Time-Speed-Distance, Profit-Loss, Algebra, Mensuration /
Subject-Verb Agreement, Tenses, Voice, Narration, Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms,
Comprehension) is already covered there — a student should **practise these in the main
Quant/Reasoning/English/Vocabulary quiz sections**, not here. Duplicating them inside the

**What stays in this module for Maths/English:** only the **Methodology of Teaching**
chapters (Direct Method, Grammar-Translation, Audio-Lingual, TPR, analytic/synthetic

which the generic quant/English pool has none of and which only TET/SGT candidates need.

The module's in-app description should point students to the main quiz app for
Maths/English skill practice, and reserve this module's own capacity for CDP/Educational
Psychology, Perspectives in Education, GK & Current Affairs, Science, Social Studies, and
Telugu — none of which the main pool covers at TET/SGT's required depth.

## Shared syllabus vs unique syllabus — read this first

TET and SGT overlap heavily but are **not identical**. To avoid confusing a student who is
prepping for both, the relationship is:

**Shared core (same content, counts toward BOTH exams)**

| Chapter group | Covers |
|---|---|
| Pedagogy (TET: "Child Development & Pedagogy" · SGT: "Educational Psychology" + "Perspectives in Education") | Piaget, Vygotsky, Erikson, Kohlberg, Intelligence, Learning theories, Memory, Personality, Individual Differences, Assessment & CCE, Guidance & Counselling, RTE 2009, RTI 2005, NCF 2005, NEP 2020, Teacher Empowerment |
| Language I (Telugu) | Grammar, vocabulary, literature, teaching methodology |
| Language II (English) | Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, teaching methodology |
| Mathematics | Number system, arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, data handling + methodology |
| Science / EVS | Living world, human body, matter, force & energy, light/heat/sound/electricity, universe + methodology |

A student who masters these chapters is prepared for the pedagogy + subject-content core
of **both** exams at once — study once, it counts for both.

**SGT (DSC)-only — TET never tests this**

| Chapter | Why TET skips it |
|---|---|
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs (16 Q / 8 marks) | TET has no GK section anywhere |
| Social Studies as a scored section (24 Q / 12 marks) | TET tests Social Studies only in Paper-2 (Cl VI–VIII teachers); SGT teaches Cl I–V, so this is genuinely new syllabus for an SGT aspirant |

**TET-only nuance** — not extra content, just different framing. TET Paper-1 turns out to
be a strict subset of SGT's syllabus: every TET topic also appears in SGT. The difference
is style — TET doesn't score methodology or GK separately, while SGT does.

Both module descriptions (shown in-app) reference this relationship so students don't
wonder why the same Piaget/RTE/NEP questions appear in two different modules, or why GK
is absent from TET.

## Module A — `ap-dsc-sgt`  ·  AP DSC — SGT (Secondary Grade Teacher)

Notification 01/Mega-DSC-TRC-1/2025 (20-Apr-2025). Selection = 80 % TRT + 20 % TET.

**Pattern:** 160 questions · 80 marks (½ mark each) · **150 minutes** · objective MCQ.

| # | Section (`subject`)              | Qs | Marks |
|---|----------------------------------|----|-------|
| 1 | General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 16 | 8  |
| 2 | Perspectives in Education        | 8  | 4  |
| 3 | Educational Psychology           | 16 | 8  |
| 4 | Language I — Telugu              | 24 | 12 |
| 5 | Language II — English           | 24 | 12 |
| 6 | Mathematics (content + methodology) | 24 | 12 |
| 7 | Science (content + methodology)  | 24 | 12 |
| 8 | Social Studies (content + methodology) | 24 | 12 |
|   | **Total**                        |160 | 80 |

SGT teaches Classes I–V, so subject content is pitched at primary / upper-primary level.

## Module B — `ap-tet`  ·  AP TET (Teacher Eligibility Test)

Notification 01-APTET-OCTOBER-2025 (24-Oct-2025). CBT. Multi-shift → normalised scores.

**Pattern:** 150 questions · 150 marks (1 mark each) · **150 minutes** · **no negative marking**.

Pass: OC-EWS ≥ 60 % (90), BC ≥ 50 % (75), SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-serv ≥ 40 % (60).

Four papers:
* **Paper-1A** (Cl I–V, general) · **Paper-1B** (Cl I–V, special schools)
* **Paper-2A** (Cl VI–VIII, general) · **Paper-2B** (Cl VI–VIII, special schools)

| Paper | Sections (30 Qs / 30 marks each)                                            |
|-------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1A/1B | Child Development & Pedagogy · Language I · Language II (English) · Mathematics · Environmental Studies |
| 2A/2B | Child Development & Pedagogy · Language I · Language II (English) · **[Math & Science] OR [Social Studies]** (60 Qs) |

Paper-1B / 2B add a Special-Education pedagogy component (autism, HI, VI, ID, LD, etc.).
